Unmoved

Privacy Policy

Unmoved collects no data. There is nothing to sign up for, no ads and no tracking.

Effective November 16, 2026. This policy covers the Unmoved app for iPhone and iPad.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, store or send any personal information to the developer or to anyone else.
  • It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and no third-party SDKs.
  • It has no accounts of its own. Game Center, described below, is optional.

What stays on your device

Your progress (solved puzzles, best Flow score) and your settings (theme, sound, vibration) are saved on your device so you can continue where you left off.

iCloud

If you use iCloud, Apple syncs your progress between your own devices through iCloud key-value storage. This data lives in your iCloud account and is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer cannot see it. You can turn this off for Unmoved in your device's iCloud settings.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, your best Flow score and the achievements you earn are sent to Apple's Game Center, where you and, depending on your Game Center settings, your friends can see them. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; the developer receives nothing from it. Unmoved never asks you to sign in to play, and you can sign out of Game Center in your device's settings.

Purchases

The one-time purchase that unlocks the full game is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer receives no payment details and no information about who you are.

Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may pass anonymous crash reports to the developer. They contain technical information about the crash only, and you can turn this off at any time under Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Unmoved is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. A change that starts collecting any data would also be shown in the app's App Store privacy details before it takes effect.
