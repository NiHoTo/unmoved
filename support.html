Support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach the developer.

How does the hold work?

Tap a numbered tile before you swipe. It stays exactly where it is during your next swipe, and every other tile moves around it as if it were a wall. After that swipe it lets go by itself. Tap it again to cancel. A swipe that moves nothing does not use up the hold.

I'm stuck on a puzzle

Hints are free and unlimited. Tap Hint below the board to see the next move. Every puzzle has been checked by a solver, so there is always a way.

What does the full game include?

The first 28 puzzles are free. One purchase unlocks puzzles 29 to 100 and Flow, the endless mode. There are no subscriptions, no ads and nothing else to buy.

I bought the full game, but it's locked

Open Settings in the app, tap The full game, then Restore purchase. Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you bought it with. With Family Sharing turned on, family members can restore it the same way.

My progress didn't follow me to another device

Progress syncs through iCloud. Check that both devices are signed in to the same iCloud account and that iCloud is on for Unmoved, then open the app on both. Syncing can take a minute.

Contact

Found a bug, or have a question that isn't answered here? Write to us on GitHub. Please include your device model and iOS version.
